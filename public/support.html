Contact

Email tinge@g.omg.lol with a description of the issue, your device model and iOS version. Please don’t send photographs or other sensitive information unless you choose to.

Photos and editing

Tinge edits photos on your device. Check your Photos permissions in iOS Settings if you cannot select or save an image. Saved copies appear in your Photos library. Photos updated in place can be restored using Photos’ Revert feature.

Looks and offline use

Installed looks and editing work offline. To manage, import, hide or delete looks, open Manage Looks in Tinge. The catalogue refresh requires an internet connection; a failed refresh leaves previously available looks usable.

Privacy and optional support

Read our Privacy Policy for information about photos, network connections and your choices. Optional tips are processed by Apple and do not unlock editing features.
