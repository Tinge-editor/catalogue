Tinge

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Tinge is a photo editor operated by George Leontiev and designed to work on your device. Your photographs are not uploaded to Tinge for editing.

Photos and edits

Tinge reads the photos you select and processes them on your device. When you save a copy, Tinge asks for permission to add it to Photos. If you choose to update an original, Tinge requests the Photos access needed to make a reversible edit; you can use Photos’ Revert feature to return to the original.

Exported images may retain metadata from the source, such as the date, camera information or location, and may include an editing note. Check a photo’s metadata before sharing it if that matters to you. If a selected photo is stored in iCloud, Apple Photos may download it to your device.

Information stored on your device

To let you resume editing offline, Tinge keeps local copies of recent source photos, thumbnails and editing settings. Tinge also stores your personal looks and preferences on your device. Recent source photos are excluded from device backups; other app data may be included in your device backup depending on your settings.

Touch and hold a recent edit and choose Remove from Recents to remove its cached source and thumbnail. You can remove personal looks in Manage Looks. Deleting Tinge removes its local app data. Photos saved to your Photos library and files you exported elsewhere remain in those locations; backups may also retain data. You can change Photos permissions in your device’s Settings.

Preset catalogues and network requests

When Tinge starts, it requests its free preset catalogue over HTTPS from GitHub Pages. The request does not include your photographs, their metadata or your edit history. GitHub says that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security when GitHub Pages is accessed. GitHub handles this connection information under its privacy statement; its retention practices apply.

You can also choose to connect to another catalogue URL. That catalogue’s host receives your network request and its own privacy practices apply. Tinge saves a validated catalogue locally so installed looks and editing remain available offline. To replace a custom catalogue, choose Connect Tinge Catalogue in the app’s catalogue options or open a local catalogue file. Tinge may still refresh its first-party catalogue at launch.

Diagnostics

Tinge does not include a third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK and does not send custom analytics events. It records limited, fixed diagnostic categories on your device when a photo import, save or catalogue refresh fails; these messages do not contain photos, file paths or the full error details.

Depending on your device settings, Apple may share app analytics or crash and performance reports with developers. You can manage analytics sharing in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. See Apple’s analytics information and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Optional tips

If available, you can choose to leave an optional one-time tip through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple handles the transaction and payment information under its own terms and privacy practices; Tinge does not receive your payment card details. Tips do not unlock editing features and are not required to use or export your photos.

Contact and your choices

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond and handle your request. You may ask about access to or deletion of information you have sent us by emailing tinge@g.omg.lol. We retain correspondence as needed to respond, maintain support records or meet legal obligations.

For information held by Apple, GitHub or another catalogue provider, their privacy policies explain their handling and available choices. Tinge does not control those providers’ retention of connection or transaction data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Tinge changes. We will publish the revised version at this address and update the date above. Review it when you update the app or use new features.
